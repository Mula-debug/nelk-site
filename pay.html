Payment Method Choice & Cryptocurrency Policy Explanation

1. Justification of Selected Payment Methods

Nelk Liquid Soap selected Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel), Card Payments via licensed payment gateways, Bank Transfers, and Cash on Delivery based on their high convenience and widespread adoption across Uganda. Mobile Money represents the primary payment standard for retail consumers in Uganda, providing instant access, high security, and seamless integration with daily mobile usage. Cash on Delivery and Direct Bank Transfers accommodate corporate, institution, and wholesale buyers who purchase liquid soap in bulk (e.g., 20-Litre Jerrycans) and require formal invoice auditing or physical confirmation prior to payment release.

2. Stance on Cryptocurrency Acceptance

While digital innovations like Bitcoin and USDT represent the "new normal" in global peer-to-peer finance, Nelk Liquid Soap currently DOES NOT accept cryptocurrency payments. This operational decision strictly aligns with official guidance issued by the Bank of Uganda (BoU).

The Bank of Uganda has explicitly cautioned the public that cryptocurrencies are not recognized as legal tender in Uganda and are not issued or backed by any regulatory central authority. Furthermore, central bank directives restrict licensed payment entities from facilitating cryptocurrency conversions or clearing crypto-related transactions due to market volatility, risk of consumer loss, and anti-money laundering concerns.

Although cryptocurrency adoption continues to grow, Nelk Liquid Soap prioritizes customer protection and legal compliance within Uganda’s financial regulatory framework. Until the Bank of Uganda and the Capital Markets Authority establish a fully licensed and supervised regulatory sandbox framework for virtual asset service providers (VASPs), our business will maintain exclusive support for official Ugandan Shilling (UGX) payment channels.
