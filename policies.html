9. Security Basics

How We Protect Our Website and Customers: We want customers to feel safe when using the Nelk Liquid Soap website. We will take practical steps to protect the website and any customer information shared with us[cite: 1].

  1. Secure Website Connection: We will use HTTPS to help protect information sent between the website and a customer’s browser. This helps keep information safer while it is being transferred[cite: 1].
  2. Protecting Customer Information: Only people who need customer information for their work should be allowed to access it. We will take reasonable steps to prevent information from being lost, misused, or seen by unauthorised people[cite: 1].
  3. Strong Passwords: We will use strong passwords for website administrator accounts and keep them private. We will change them if we think someone else may know them[cite: 1].
  4. Safe Payments: If customers pay through our website, we will use a trusted payment service. We will avoid keeping customers’ bank card details on our own website[cite: 1].
  5. Website Updates: We will keep the website software and security features updated to help reduce security risks[cite: 1].
  6. Responding to Security Problems: If we find a security problem that may affect customers, we will investigate it and work to fix it. If necessary, we will inform the affected customers[cite: 1].

Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting our website and our customers’ information. We will check our security measures regularly and improve them when needed[cite: 1].
